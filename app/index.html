Roadmap to the platinum

PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10 and about 200 hours in one playthrough; Push Square says to set aside up to 300 hours or more. There are no difficulty settings, no mission replay and no online trophies — and free roam stays open after the story, so almost everything can wait until the ending.

  1. Step 1 — Play the story, take side missions as they come 60–80 hours

    Finish the five chapters and pick up optional missions as they appear (purple icons, plus black icons with pink letters). Scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app and study animals through the binoculars as you go — the 100% checklist wants 200 of 277 vehicle models and 100 of 255 animals, and everything you do before the ending already counts.

  2. Step 2 — The 100% checklist and the challenges about 100 hours

    After the story, open Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion. The requirements are lenient — PowerPyx calls it closer to 60% true completion — but you do need every robbery, underwater discovery, shooting range, drone race and Bret quest, plus 70 of the 80 challenges, so you can skip the hardest ones.

  3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and the leftovers about 10 hours

    Finish the 8 stranger mission strands and whatever trophies did not pop on their own. The checklist does not ask for these strands, so they are usually what is left at the end.

The one trophy you can miss: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots. The first meditation is offered at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth: Lucia mentions meditating in the kitchen one morning and walks to the balcony. Follow her and choose Accept. It is gone after Chapter 1, so do it the moment it appears.

Save smart: there is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter — PowerPyx suggests 10 of your 15 manual slots for that, and the other 5 for free-roam cleanup.

Cheats off: cheats disable trophies and saving, and they only unlock after the story — exactly when most cleanup happens. Keep them off the save you are using for the platinum.

Still unsolved — no verified method yet

Four trophies had no confirmed unlock method in any guide checked on 21 November 2026, two days after launch. This guide says so plainly instead of inventing steps, and will update as the community cracks them:

  • In The Beginning… — enjoy a cocktail with a view on Ocean Beach.
  • Capital Offense — smash your way out of a vehicle showroom in classic style. (GTABase suggests crashing out through a showroom window — unconfirmed.)
  • Bad Luck, I Guess — “Second time’s the charm.”
  • The Truth Is Out There — “What the hell just happened?” (GTABase suggests a strange event somewhere in Leonida — unconfirmed.)

Two more are single-source reports, not yet widely confirmed: Personal Best (GTABase: max out Strength for both characters) and Ten Pin (a melee drive-by — exact trigger unconfirmed). Their guides say so.
